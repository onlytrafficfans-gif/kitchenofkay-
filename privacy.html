Your information

Privacy

Kitchen of Kay collects only the information needed to prepare, pay for, and confirm a pickup order.

Effective November 18, 2026

Information you enter

Your pickup name, order selections, and kitchen note are saved in your browser so your draft survives a refresh. When you choose secure checkout, those order details are sent to Kitchen of Kay through Stripe so the kitchen can prepare and confirm your pickup.

Text messages and calls

Call and text buttons open your device’s phone or messaging app. Your wireless provider and messaging app may process those communications under their own privacy terms.

Payments

Online payments are processed by Stripe. Kitchen of Kay does not receive or store your full card number. Stripe may process your contact, billing, payment, and device information under its own privacy policy.

Accounts and analytics

The website does not create customer accounts, use advertising trackers, or run third-party analytics.

Stored website data

The site uses local browser storage for your order draft and motion preference. The installable app uses a service-worker cache so previously opened pages and images can work offline. You can clear this data through your browser settings.
