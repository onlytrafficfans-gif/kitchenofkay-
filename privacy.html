Your information

Privacy

The current Kitchen of Kay website is designed to collect as little information as possible while helping you prepare a pickup request.

Effective October 10, 2026

Information you enter

Your pickup name, order selections, and kitchen note are saved only in your browser so your draft survives a refresh. Kitchen of Kay does not receive that information until you choose to send the prepared text message.

Text messages and calls

Call and text buttons open your device’s phone or messaging app. Your wireless provider and messaging app may process those communications under their own privacy terms.

Payments, accounts, and analytics

This version of the website does not take online payments, create customer accounts, use advertising trackers, or run third-party analytics. This notice will need to be updated before payment, email, account, or analytics services are enabled.

Stored website data

The site uses local browser storage for your order draft and motion preference. The installable app uses a service-worker cache so previously opened pages and images can work offline. You can clear this data through your browser settings.

Questions

For a privacy question, call or text Kitchen of Kay at (747) 352-6722.
