About the Chef

Cooking has always meant more to me than just preparing a meal—it’s how I connect, give back, and bring joy to others. I grew up in a home where superb cooking was simply a part of life, so sharing that tradition feels natural. Kitchen of Kay was created from the fulfillment I feel when someone enjoys the food I make for them. Every dish is prepared with heart, purpose, and a desire to bring people together.
